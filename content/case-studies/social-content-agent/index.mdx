## Where I started

Generating a draft is one thing. Deciding whether it should become a published post is another.

This case study explores the space between those two moments: the editing, review, and deliberate decisions that give a content workflow its shape.

<Callout title="About these notes">
This is a draft framing of Social Content Agent, based on the project handoff. The interface above is a concept illustration. The implementation, architecture, and decision rationale still need to be checked against the real project.
</Callout>

## Following the workflow

The described workflow moves from AI-assisted drafting through human editing and approval to scheduling and activity history. The key question is where a person can inspect, change, or stop the next action.

<WorkflowSteps>
  <Step title="Start with a draft">Generate material that a person can work with, rather than treating the first response as a final post.</Step>
  <Step title="Make room for judgment">Read, edit, and review the content before deciding whether it is ready.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose what happens next">Approve and schedule deliberately. Publishing in the described version is simulated.</Step>
</WorkflowSteps>

<Callout title="A deliberate boundary">
Scheduling does not establish that a live social integration exists. This study will retain the simulated-publishing disclosure unless project evidence confirms a change.
</Callout>
