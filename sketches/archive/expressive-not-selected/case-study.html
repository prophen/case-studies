01 / Problem & scope

A draft is not a decision.

Generating content is only part of the experience. The harder product question is how people review it, change it, and decide what happens next.

Publishing is simulated in this version.

The case study should separate the review and scheduling experience from external social publishing. No live integration or impact metrics are implied here.

04 / Technical decisions

Explain the choices, not just the stack.

Proposed narrative / verify against implementation

Make approval an explicit transition.

Decision
Distinguish an editable draft from content a person has approved.
Why
Generation should not silently become permission to take an external action.
Tradeoff
An additional review step adds friction, but makes intent easier to understand.
Evidence
Attach the actual transition handler, validation rules, and a screenshot of the approval interaction.
What the final evidence should answer

Can an unapproved draft be scheduled? What happens if approved content changes? Which checks live on the server? Document observed behavior rather than assuming the safeguards exist.
